# BRAINSTORM - Webmaster

## What is the site?

A free site where any high school student can go from “what is ai” to using it honestly and ethically in a school setting, in around an hour, and has fun doing so.

## What is the PURPOSE of the site?

To teach high school students about AI, a foundational understanding of it, practical tools, and how to leverage AI effectively and ethically in an academic setting. Anything in the website that does not contribute to this goal is unnecessary, as it does not support the broad purpose.

## Who is it for?

9th grader who uses ChatGPT and doesnt know how it works or ethical uses of it. Also, a judge who gives the site 10 minutes. Broader, it is targeted toward high school students. Today’s high school students have low attention spans and are used to social media. Thinking about this, we could get the videos to be in a shorts feed type thing. This will change the structure of the module.

## What should the student walk away knowing?

- Fundamentals of AI
- Practical AI Tools
- Ethical AI usage

## What is inside of each module?

- A short video. Scroll Down
- An interactive way to participate in that concept themselves.
- A quick 5-question quiz over the lesson

The above is a lesson. Each module will have a variety of lessons. They are connected via a roadmap, similar to Candy Crush.

## What makes it fun?

- XP
- Climbing rank
- Certificate
- Progress Page
- Badge collection for each lesson that is passed you get a badge, and you can see your badges on the progress page.

## What makes our website different?

- Hands-on activities
- Fun, extremely high-quality videos
- Specifically targeted short form content for our target audience.
- The site practices what it teaches. The site is about AI usage, and we can exemplify that through the decisions and development of the site!
- Intense user testing and validation
- Every fact is quoted in the video with a reliable source
- Every question is quoted from the video or activity.
- Not sad activities that are really not fun. They need some jam.

## What does the judge see in the first 60 seconds?

A clear CTA “Start Learning”
A beautiful website
They click start learning, and see the roadmap
Dont do overcomplicated setup or anything
